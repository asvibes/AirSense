AirSense

AirSense turns indoor CO₂ readings into room trends and advisory alerts, so your household knows when to open a window. Simple for families, built carefully for reviewers.

Features ​

01Rooms & ventilationRooms & ventilation

Add the rooms in your home and tick the ventilation sources each one has, like windows and fans.

02Sensor provisioningSensor provisioning

Owners provision a sensor per room. Its device key is shown once and only a peppered hash is stored. Rotate or revoke keys anytime.

03DashboardDashboard

Room cards with the latest values and open alerts, a latest-reading chart, and a light or dark theme.

04History & CSV exportHistory & CSV export

Filter readings by date, room and metric, scan a 90-day coverage heatmap, and export to CSV.

05Household rolesHousehold roles

Owners manage rooms, sensors, thresholds and single-use invitations. Members can view home data and submit simulated readings.

06Demo labDemo lab

No hardware needed: submit simulated readings or replay a processed DALTON CO₂ CSV through the same alert pipeline.

07Built with careBuilt with care

Flask, SQLAlchemy and Alembic migrations. Scrypt password hashing, CSRF protection, rate-limited login, an owner-only audit log and an automated test suite.

Alert flow Alert flow

01Detect

High

A reading crosses the CO₂ threshold set for that room.

02Detect

Sustained elevated

Levels stay above the elevated mark for a sustained period.

03Detect

Rapid rise

CO₂ climbs quickly, even before a threshold is reached.

04Resolve

Acknowledge & clear

One open alert per room. Acknowledge it, ventilate, and an all-clear follows after a quiet period.

AirSense is a ventilation-support demo, not a medical or diagnostic system. It reads configured sensors or simulator values, thresholds are illustrative, and it does not replace a certified carbon monoxide alarm.
